Before you upload

  1. No names, anywhere. Not in the ID, file name, title or codes. The record is identified only by its record ID.
  2. One learner per upload. Put all pages of one script into a single PDF, in order.
  3. Clear scans. Flat page, whole page in view, about 300 dpi, no shadows. Crossed-out words are detected and left out automatically.
  4. IDs are never reused. Each record gets a readable ID (LEVEL-YEAR-TASK-NNN) plus a hidden unique key. You'll be warned if the ID or the same file was saved before.
  5. You can stop at any time. Work saves automatically in this browser. Come back to this page later, on the same computer and browser, and choose Resume where I left off.
